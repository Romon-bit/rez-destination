ABOUT MALÉ

About Malé

Malé is one of the most densely populated cities in the world, packed onto a small island in the North Malé Atoll. Most visitors pass through it on arrival, but it rewards a half-day or full day on foot. You can see the golden-domed Islamic Centre, browse the fish market and local shops, and get a feel for everyday Maldivian life before heading to the resorts.

🕌

Islamic Centre

The golden-domed Grand Friday Mosque is the city's best-known landmark.

🐟

Fish Market and Waterfront

🌳

Sultan Park and Museum

A quiet green space beside the National Museum, good for a break from the streets.

✈️ Travel tips

Malé is walkable in a few hours. Dress modestly in the city, avoid the midday heat, and note that Friday is the main prayer day, so some places open later.
