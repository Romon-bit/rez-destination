BETWEEN TWO LAKES

Discover Interlaken 🏞️

Interlaken sits between Lake Thun and Lake Brienz, with the Bernese Alps rising behind it.

Explore ↓
ABOUT INTERLAKEN

About Interlaken

The town is a base for the Jungfrau region and for outdoor activities such as hiking, paragliding and boat trips. Its name means "between the lakes".

🌄

Harder Kulm

A funicular ride to a viewpoint over both lakes and the Alps.

🛶

Lake Thun and Lake Brienz

Cruise or walk the shores of two clear alpine lakes.

🪂

Adventure Activities

Paragliding, canyoning and hiking are popular here.
