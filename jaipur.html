THE PINK CITY

About Jaipur Jaipur 🏰

Jaipur, the capital of Rajasthan, is famous for its pink-coloured historic buildings, royal palaces, grand forts and lively traditional markets. The city blends Rajput heritage with colourful crafts, local food and welcoming culture.

🏰

Amber Fort

Explore a grand hilltop fort known for its courtyards, detailed architecture and views of the surrounding landscape.

🪟

Hawa Mahal

Admire the iconic Palace of Winds, recognised for its distinctive pink façade and rows of small windows.

👑

City Palace

Discover royal courtyards, museums and architecture reflecting Jaipur's historic royal family.

✈️ Travel Tips

Wear comfortable shoes while exploring forts and markets. Visit popular monuments earlier in the day and ask permission before photographing local people.
