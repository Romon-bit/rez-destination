THE CITY OF PEARLS

Welcome to Hyderabad

Hyderabad is the capital of Telangana, India. It is well known for its rich Nizam history, beautiful architecture, traditional bazaars and famous cuisine. Visitors can experience historic landmarks alongside modern attractions, shopping centres and lively cultural spaces.

EXPLORE THE CITY

Famous Places in Hyderabad

🕌

Charminar

Hyderabad's iconic monument, known for its four grand minarets and the lively markets surrounding it.

🏰

Golconda Fort

A historic fortress famous for its impressive architecture, massive gateways and fascinating past.

🎬

Ramoji Film City

A popular film studio complex with movie sets, entertainment attractions and guided tours.

🌊

Hussain Sagar Lake

A scenic lake featuring a large Buddha statue and pleasant waterfront views.

🏛️

Chowmahalla Palace

A magnificent palace showcasing the royal lifestyle, elegant halls and heritage of the Nizams.

🍛

Hyderabadi Biryani

Taste the city's famous aromatic rice dish, prepared with traditional spices and local flavours.

Travel Tips

☀️ Best time: October to March.

🚇 Getting around: Metro, buses, taxis and auto-rickshaws.

🍽️ Must try: Hyderabadi biryani and local street food.

📸 Don't miss: Charminar, Golconda Fort and Hussain Sagar Lake.
