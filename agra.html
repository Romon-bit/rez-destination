THE CITY OF THE TAJ

About Agra

Agra is a historic city on the banks of the Yamuna River in Uttar Pradesh, India. It flourished under the Mughal Empire and is famous for its spectacular monuments, intricate architecture, traditional crafts and rich culinary heritage. Visitors can explore centuries of history while experiencing the city's lively markets and local culture.

Famous Attractions

🤍

Taj Mahal

A world-famous white marble mausoleum commissioned by Emperor Shah Jahan. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🏰

Agra Fort

A magnificent historic fort with red sandstone walls, palaces and courtyards. It is also a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🕌

Fatehpur Sikri

A historic Mughal city near Agra, known for its grand gateways, courtyards and remarkable architecture.

✈️ Travel Tips

• Visit popular monuments early to enjoy a more comfortable visit.
• Check monument opening days and ticket details before travelling.
• Carry water and comfortable walking shoes.
• Respect local customs and follow photography rules at attractions.
